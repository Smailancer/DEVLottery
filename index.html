Methodology & Sources

EARN (40%)

100 = Direct Stripe; 75 = Stripe with restrictions/local partner (Paystack, Invite-only Preview); 60 = PayPal can receive; 30 = Paddle (MoR) only; 0 = Sanctioned / no reliable rail. Sources: stripe.com/global, Paddle supported countries, PayPal docs.

BUY (40%)

Work hours required for a $20/mo dev tool (or $80/mo AI stack in the simulator), using World Bank GNI per capita ÷ 12 at market FX where parallel rates exist (e.g. Algeria Square rate). Log-scaled with fixed goalposts (0.38h best, 71h worst).

MOVE (20%)

Visa-free destinations from Henley Passport Index 2026 (23–192 scale), plus Bali/Indonesia entry policy & US Visa Waiver (ESTA) eligibility. Birth odds: UN World Population Prospects 2024.

Known limitations: GNI per capita is a national baseline, not a senior developer salary (Stack Overflow salary overlay planned). Some countries still use official FX where market FX is unsourced. Birth draw weights reflect the 33 countries included in v2.
